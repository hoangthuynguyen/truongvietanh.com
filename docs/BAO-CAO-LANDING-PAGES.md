## 10. TOI UU DA THUC HIEN

### SEO
- Title + Meta description tren tat ca cac trang
- Canonical URL cho moi trang
- Structured data: EducationalOrganization, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, CollectionPage, Article
- Internal links cheo giua cac pillar pages (topic cluster)
- Sitemap tu dong (astro-sitemap)
- robots.txt
- noindex cho staging/workers.dev/cam-on pages

### AIO (AI Overviews)
- Cau tra loi truc tiep trong 2-3 cau dau moi H2
- FAQ schema tren tat ca pillar pages
- Noi dung cu the voi so lieu (IELTS 6.0-8.0, 99% dai hoc, 9 co so...)

### Load nhanh
- Anh WebP toi uu (max 1200px, quality 82%)
- loading="lazy" tren tat ca anh
- Font async loading (Inter, Montserrat)
- Static output (pre-rendered HTML)
- Cloudflare Workers edge delivery

### Lead tuyen sinh
- LeadFormTwoStepEmailFirst tren 24+ trang
- 2-step form (email first → full info)
- Hotline 0916 961 409 + Zalo tren 100% trang
- Thank you pages voi countdown + CTA tiep theo
- GTM tracking (GTM-KPL2NSR9) tren 100% trang
- Webhook → Make.com → GHL + Pancake CRM
