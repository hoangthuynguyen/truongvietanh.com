5 KHẮC PHỤC Thói quen 4 — cùng thắngTự hỏi: Cách sửa này qua được 5 câu hỏi vàng không?Nói: "Em nghĩ mình có thể làm gì để sửa chuyện này?" (đưa 2–3 lựa chọn)Từ chối hợp tác → "Mình nói lại khi cả hai bình tĩnh" + góc bình tĩnh.

Khi học sinh từ chối hợp tác: rút khỏi cuộc đấu quyền lực, không rút khỏi học sinh. "Thầy/cô sẽ nói với em khi cả hai bình tĩnh — lúc ra chơi hay cuối tiết, em chọn." Tiếp tục bài dạy. Không tranh luận, không đe doạ, không lôi cả lớp vào.

5 GOLDEN QUESTIONS — before any repair action EXCELLENCE — Will the student learn something? RESPONSIBILITY — Is it reasonably related to the behaviour? RESPECT — Does it protect the student's dignity? LOVE — Is it physically and emotionally safe? INTEGRITY — Is it reasonable and fair for the age, circumstances and ability? One "No" → don't do it. Sixth check: would I explain this to the parents and the Chairman? 6 STEPS — Viet Anh Positive Discipline 1 PAUSE — safety, calm yourself 2 CONNECT — deposit before withdrawal 3 LISTEN WITH EMPATHY — "What happened?" 4 SHARE — "What's our rule here?" 5 REPAIR — "What could you do to make this right?" 6 WALK ALONGSIDE — check back; F01 if Level 2+ Red lines: no exercise, no pain, no shame, no collective punishment, no withholding basic needs.

Phần 4 — Họp lớp 15 phút, mỗi tuần một lần

Họp lớp là nơi lớp tự giải quyết phần lớn vấn đề Mức 1–2 mà không cần giáo viên đơn phương xử lý, và là nơi học sinh tập kỹ năng lãnh đạo. Chọn cố định một khung Morning/Advisory Meeting trong tuần (đề xuất thứ Sáu). Giáo viên chủ nhiệm chủ trì; từ lớp 4, học sinh luân phiên làm chủ toạ, thư ký, người giữ giờ.

Phiên bản theo cấp

Lớp 1–2: 10 phút, giáo viên chủ trì, mỗi lần một vấn đề, giải pháp bằng tranh; vòng ghi nhận dùng gấu bông chuyền tay.

Lớp 3–5: 15 phút, học sinh bắt đầu làm thư ký và người giữ giờ; giáo viên chủ toạ.

THCS–THPT: 15 phút, học sinh chủ toạ luân phiên hai tuần một lần; giáo viên chỉ can thiệp khi vi phạm luật họp; nội dung khó (bắt nạt, xung đột kéo dài) không đưa ra họp lớp mà chuyển tầng 2.

Nội trú: họp phòng 10 phút tối Chủ nhật do quản nhiệm chủ trì, cùng cấu trúc.

Ba lỗi làm họp lớp chết: (1) giáo viên biến thành giờ nhắc nhở; (2) lớp chọn "phạt" bạn làm giải pháp; (3) không kiểm tra lại giải pháp tuần trước. Tránh được ba lỗi này thì tuần thứ tư trở đi lớp tự chạy.

For native-speaking teachers. The Vietnamese text prevails.

Part 1 — The 6-step flow (A4 wall poster)

Target for an ordinary situation: 1–3 minutes. Read top to bottom; the right-hand column tells you where to branch.

1 PAUSE Habit 1 — the pause buttonTự hỏi: Is anyone unsafe? Am I calm?Nói: "Stop for a moment. I need you safe first."Not calm → come back later. Risk → report to Campus Leader now.
2 CONNECT Deposit in the emotional bank accountTự hỏi: Does the student feel seen?Nói: "I can see you're upset. I'm here with you."No blame, no old mistakes.
3 LISTEN WITH EMPATHY Habit 5 — seek first to understandTự hỏi: Deliberate – careless – didn't know – lacks skill – circumstances?Nói: "What happened?" "What happened right before?" "What makes this hard for you?"Avoid "why". Didn't know → go to step 4 and teach.
4 SHARE Habit 2 — begin with the end in mindTự hỏi: Does the student know the expectation?Nói: "What's our rule here?" "Who was affected?"Didn't know → teach, don't correct. Knew → step 5.
5 REPAIR Habit 4 — think win-winTự hỏi: Does this repair pass the 5 Golden Questions?Nói: "What could you do to make this right?" (offer 2–3 options)Refuses → "We'll talk when we're both calm" + calm-down corner.
6 WALK ALONGSIDE Habit 3 + PDRTự hỏi: When do I check back? Who needs to know?Nói: "Let's check on Friday."Level 2+ or repeat → F01. 2nd repeat → Plan B within 3 days. 3rd → homeroom teacher, Tier 2.
If a student refuses to cooperate: step out of the power struggle, not away from the student. "I'll talk with you when we're both calm — at break or after the lesson, you choose." Keep teaching. No arguing, no threats, no involving the class.

Quick read: what is the behaviour saying? (after Dreikurs)

I feel…The student may be seeking…Hidden messageWhat works
Annoyed, irritatedAttention"Notice me."Notice them doing right; give a useful job; a private signal instead of verbal reminders.
Challenged, threatenedPower"Let me have a say."Don't fight; limited choices; ask for help; talk later.
Hurt, disappointedRevenge"I'm hurting. See my hurt."Don't retaliate; acknowledge feelings; repair the relationship first, the behaviour second.

Teaching schedule

Foundation week 1: classroom, corridors. Week 2: canteen, playground and PE. Week 3: bus, boarding, digital. Week 4: review and the class agreement. Re-teach all settings in one week after Tết, at the start of semester 2 and after summer, and whenever F01 data shows a rise in one setting. Lesson format, 15 minutes: Tell → Show (right and wrong, teacher acts it out) → Practise on site → Specific acknowledgement. Ratio: at least four specific acknowledgements for every correction.

Part 3 — The 15-minute weekly class meeting

MinBeatHowRules
0–3AppreciationsSit in a circle. Each student (or 8–10 in rotation) says one sentence: "I thank … for …". Teacher goes last.Speak to the person, not about them. No one is skipped for a whole month.
3–5Check last week's solutionSecretary reads the chosen solution; hands up: better / not yet. Not yet → stays on the agenda.No blame; only "did the solution work?"
5–12Agenda bookAnyone can write an issue in the agenda book during the week. Chair reads the first 1–2. Brief statement; class brainstorms (secretary writes everything down); choose one solution to try for a week.Issues about an individual are stated without names. Every solution passes the 5 Golden Questions — no solution is a punishment. The person affected chooses.
12–15CloseRe-read the class agreement; pick one focus for next week; short closing commitment.The teacher does not use these 3 minutes for reminders.

Grades 1–2: 10 minutes, teacher chairs, one issue at a time. Grades 3–5: students begin as secretary and timekeeper. Secondary: students chair in rotation; serious matters (bullying, prolonged conflict) go to Tier 2, not to the meeting. Three mistakes that kill a class meeting: turning it into a lecture, letting the class choose a punishment, and not checking last week's solution.
